Commodity Flow

When Prices Embed Logistics

Prices do not move commodities.
Systems do.

What we observe in LNG and oil markets is not just price fluctuation. It is the manifestation of constraints embedded in global infrastructure.

Using UN Comtrade data, I constructed a simple CIF–FOB unit value proxy. This is not a freight rate. It is a structural signal.

  • 2020: Collapse — demand shock
  • 2022: Spike — system under stress
  • 2023–2024: Normalization

Oil flows through a flexible system. LNG does not.

LNG is constrained by infrastructure, vessels, and contracts.

Prices tell us what changed.
Flows explain why.
Structures define what is possible.
