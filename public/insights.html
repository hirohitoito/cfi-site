When Prices Embed Logistics

Commodity prices do not move alone.

They embed logistics, infrastructure constraints, and the physical limits of global trade systems.

Using UN Comtrade data, I constructed a simple CIF–FOB proxy to capture the logistics pressure embedded in LNG and oil markets.

The pattern is clear:

  • 2020: Collapse (demand shock)
  • 2022: Spike (system stress)
  • 2023–2024: Normalization

LNG behaves differently from oil.

It is constrained by liquefaction capacity, vessels, terminals, and long-term contracts. Oil is more flexible.

This means substitution is not just about price — it is about structure.

Prices tell us what changed.
Flows explain why.
Structures indicate what comes next.
