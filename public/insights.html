When Prices Embed Logistics: A Structural View of LNG and Oil

Commodity prices do not move alone.

They often embed logistics, infrastructure constraints, and the physical limitations of global trade systems.

As an initial signal for Commodity Flow, I constructed a simple CIF–FOB unit value proxy using UN Comtrade data.

The result shows a clear pattern:

  • Collapse in 2020
  • Spike in 2022
  • Normalization in 2023–2024

LNG behaves differently from oil. It is more constrained by infrastructure, contracts, and specialized logistics.

This means substitution is not just about price. It is about structure.

Prices tell us what changed. Flows explain why. Structures indicate what comes next.
